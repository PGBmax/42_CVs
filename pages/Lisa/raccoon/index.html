The Raccoon

Welcome, find out just how cool the raccoon is

Cross-eyed

The raccoon, an animal native to the Americas, is mainly nocturnal. Although it has good night vision, this is significantly reduced during the day. It is short-sighted and has poor distance vision; furthermore, it is colour-blind, which makes it difficult for it to distinguish between colours.

Agile

The raccoon is an agile species that handles its food carefully before eating it. It was once thought that it washed its food, but in reality, this is not the case; it compensates for its poor daytime vision through touch. Some studies in captivity have even shown that it is capable of opening latches and therefore possesses a fairly well-developed cognitive ability.

Solitary

Although solitary by nature, the raccoon can nevertheless be seen in pairs for a few weeks during the breeding season. Although it does not hibernate, during cold, snowy periods its metabolism slows down, and it sleeps for long periods, living off its fat reserves. As a result, it can lose up to 50 per cent of its body weight during these difficult times.

Average

The raccoon, with an average lifespan spanning several years, remains a fascinating species to observe and study. It is also interesting to note that certain exotic species of raccoon have been introduced into various towns in France, adding a unique touch to the local wildlife.
